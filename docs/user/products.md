# Products and coupons

## Products

Open **Product → Products** and press **Add product**.

1. Choose the organisation, give a name and type (membership, ticket, merchandise, donation, other) and the price.
2. **Status:** set **Inactive** to stop selling it. Products aren't deleted, because past purchases point at them.

Membership plans and event tickets each use one product for their price.

## Discounts and coupon codes

Open **Product → Discounts & coupons** and press **Add discount**.

- **Kind:** percent off (e.g. 20) or a fixed amount off (e.g. 500).
- **Coupon code:**
  - leave it **empty** for an automatic discount (e.g. "Early bird"), applied to everyone while it's valid;
  - or enter a code (e.g. `ALUMNI20`) that people type in. Codes ignore upper and lower case.
- **Applies to:** one product, every product of a type, or (both empty) all your organisation's products.
- **Valid from / until**, **Uses in total**, **Uses per person:** leave empty for no limit.

When several discounts apply, people get the one that saves them the most. The **Used** column shows how often a discount was used.
